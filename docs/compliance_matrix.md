## Three Highest‑Priority Gaps Before Submission
1. **Missing ground truth and accuracy evaluation** – Without a benchmark dataset with known measurements (laser/tape ground truth) and accuracy evaluation, we cannot meet accuracy and repeatability gates.
2. **Lack of capture pipeline and hardware integration** – The assignment expects a capture route (your own iOS app or off‑the‑shelf protocol) and a device matrix; we have only inspected existing datasets.
3. **No unified output contract and room plan** – The final deliverable requires a dimensioned per‑room plan, stitched multi‑room plan with correct adjacency, per‑surface damage regions, concealed‑damage flags, scope line items, confidence intervals, and rendered plan; we have only raw point clouds and experiment‑specific JSON/CSV.
